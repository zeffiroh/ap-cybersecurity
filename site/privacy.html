Privacy notice

Aayat-AP-Prep · Last updated 24 November 2026

This notice explains what Aayat-AP-Prep keeps about you, why, who else handles it, and how to see, correct or delete it. Aayat-AP-Prep is run by Mitali Aayat Mahnoor Sharma, New Delhi, India ("we"). We are the Data Fiduciary for your personal data under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

What we keep, and why

What Why When
Your email address To make your account, sign you in, send password-reset codes, and link what you bought to you When you sign up or buy
Your password, scrambled (we never see or store the password itself) To sign you in When you sign up
Sign-in records (a scrambled token per signed-in browser) To keep you signed in Each sign-in
Quiz and test answers, scores and feedback To grade your work and show your progress When you answer
Learning activity: which lessons, labs and videos you opened, and on which day To show your progress, and for your teacher to see who needs help As you use the site
Notebooks, lab work and hand-ins you send To grade them and give feedback When you hand in
Forum posts, live-class bookings and attendance To run the forum and the classes When you post or book
CTF answers and scores To run the scoreboard When you submit
Seminar progress and certificates (the name you type on the certificate) To issue certificates and let an employer check one When you finish a seminar
Payment records: email, amount, currency, method (for example "card" or "UPI"), the store's order number To give you what you bought, handle refunds and keep the accounts the law requires When you pay
Ticket balance To book classes and seminars When you buy or spend tickets

We do not receive or keep card numbers, UPI PINs or bank details; the payment companies below handle those. Grades are private: you see only your own; your teacher sees their students'.

What stays on your own device

Notes, lab work in progress and some settings are kept in your browser's storage on your own computer, not on our servers. Clearing your browser data deletes them. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

Who else handles your data

Company What for Where
Neon Our database (everything in the table above) Brazil (São Paulo)
GitHub Hosts the website (its servers log visitors' IP addresses) and stores our encrypted weekly database backups United States
Razorpay Payments in India India
Gumroad Payments from outside India United States
Google (Gmail) Sends password-reset codes, unlock codes, and emails about what you bought, your classes and feedback on your hand-ins United States
YouTube (privacy-enhanced player, youtube-nocookie.com) Plays lesson and seminar videos inside our pages United States

Each of these has its own privacy policy. We don't sell or rent your data, and we don't share it with advertisers.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and learning records: while your account is open, then deleted within 30 days of a deletion request.
  • Backups: the database is copied once a week into an encrypted backup, and each backup is kept for 90 days. After we delete your data, it disappears from the backups as they expire, within 90 days.
  • Payment records: as long as Indian tax and GST law requires, even after an account is deleted.
  • Sign-in tokens and password-reset codes: until you sign out or the code expires.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what we keep about you
  • correct it
  • delete it (except what the law makes us keep)

When we delete your account, we keep only your payment records, your ticket record and the record of what each payment unlocked (switched off), because tax law requires them. Forum threads that other people replied to stay, with your name and words removed.

You can also name someone to act for you if you can't. Email aayatmahnoor@proton.me from the address on your account, with the subject "My data". We answer within 7 days and finish within 30.

If you're not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Learners under 18

Learners under 18 use the site the same way as everyone else. We use learning activity only to show progress to you and your teacher, never for advertising or to build a profile of you for anyone else.

Security

Passwords are stored only as a scrambled hash. The site uses HTTPS. Teacher access needs a teacher account or key. Backups are encrypted before they leave the database. If a breach puts your data at risk, we'll tell you and the Data Protection Board as the law requires.

Changes

If we change this notice, we'll change the date at the top, and email account holders if the change matters.
