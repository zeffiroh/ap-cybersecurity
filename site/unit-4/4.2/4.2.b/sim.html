Login Attempt Review

Review each reported login attempt in the security terminal. Judge whether it satisfies TRUE multi-factor authentication (two or more DIFFERENT factor categories), or if it is effectively SINGLE-FACTOR, or if it is multi-factor but PHISHABLE (relies on SMS/OTP relay or unverified push approval rather than a phishing-resistant factor). Achieve a perfect score of 5 / 5 to complete the drill.
