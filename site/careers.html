How every seminar works

1. Watch

A recorded seminar with chapters, and the same steps as a written tutorial.

2. Do it with me

Walkthrough: every step done in front of you, the tools open with the model answer.

3. Do it yourself

Practice with hints, then Solo with none, in the Hacker Room.

4. Hand it in

The document a job expects: a triage note, a rule pack, an incident report. Graded with a rubric.

5. Get certified

A certificate with hours and a verification number, for your CV, LinkedIn or CPE self-reporting.

Questions

Do I need to know Linux or programming first?

No. The free seminar and seminar 1 start from reading files and logs. Every command is explained.

Is the certificate accredited?

No. It records what you did, the hours and the date, with a number anyone can verify. Many certification bodies let you self-report the hours as CPE; acceptance is their decision.

Are these real tools?

Some are the real thing running in your browser (YARA-X, Sigma with pySigma, Python, SQL, CyberChef, Linux). Others are our own simulators that use the real syntax and output, with our own look. Some seminars read saved output from tools that cannot run in a browser.
