Teacher case: insider and access control guide

Who changed the grades?

Beginner · about 30 min

Grades changed under a teacher's name, in subjects he doesn't teach, from a student lab PC. Use the audit log, sign-in records and lab logins to find out how, and fix the access control.

Worked exampleGuidedOn your own

Tuesday 10 Nov. At Westbrook High, a Chemistry teacher says grades were changed under his name in subjects he doesn't even teach. The audit log agrees it was his account. Was it him, or someone using his access?

The evidence is in ~/case (Linux) and case/ (Python). The network tools (nmap, curl, dig, whois, openssl and more) work on a recorded copy of the case network; nothing leaves your browser.

What you'll learn

  • Use an audit log to answer who, what, when and from where
  • Tie an account to a person with a second source (lab logins)
  • Spot access control failures: no least privilege, no approval, remember-me on shared PCs

How to use this runbook

  • Run a cell with Shift+Enter (or its Run button). Run the cells in order: the first Linux cell copies the evidence into ~/case.
  • Tick each - [ ] box when you have done it.
  • Stuck? Open a Hint under the step.
  • Type your answer into each check box and it tells you if you are right. Answers are short: an address, a name, a number or a time such as 09:05.
  • Write your notes under each step. Download .ipynb keeps your notes and every output.

import csv

ev = []
for r in csv.DictReader(open("case/lab_logins.csv")):
    if r["host"] == "lab3-pc27":
        ev.append((r["time"][11:19], "lab", r["user"] + " " + r["event"]))
for line in open("case/sso.log"):
    if "lab3-pc27" in line:
        f = dict(x.split("=", 1) for x in line.split()[1:])
        ev.append((line[11:19], "sso", f["user"] + " " + f["method"]))
for r in csv.DictReader(open("case/grade_audit.csv")):
    if r["source_ip"] == "10.80.3.27":
        ev.append((r["time"][11:19], "grade", r["student"] + " " + r["course"] + " " + r["old"] + " -> " + r["new"]))
for t, src, what in sorted(ev):
    print(t, src.ljust(6), what)

5. Fix the access control

  • ☐ Restore the five grades from the audit log; tell the head teacher, not the student first (school policy and fairness)
  • ☐ Least privilege: a teacher can only change grades in classes they teach
  • ☐ Separation of duties: big changes (two grades or more) need a second person to approve
  • ☐ No remember-me on shared lab PCs; sessions end at logoff; MFA for every grade change
  • ☐ Alert when grades change from a student lab address
