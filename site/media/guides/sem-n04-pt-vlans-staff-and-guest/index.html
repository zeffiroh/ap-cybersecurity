Network lab 4: VLANs for staff and guests (Packet Tracer)

Intermediate · about 45 min

Split one switch into a staff VLAN and a guest VLAN with real switch commands, and prove that guests can't reach staff computers.

WalkthroughPracticeSolo

The files for this seminar are in ~/case (Linux) and case/ (Python): README.txt.

1. Install Packet Tracer and build the office

Cisco Packet Tracer is free with a Cisco Networking Academy account. Sign up at https://www.netacad.com, enrol in the free "Getting Started with Cisco Packet Tracer" course, and download it from the course page (Windows, macOS or Linux).

Place one 2960 switch and four PCs. Cable them with Copper Straight-Through to the ports in README.txt. On each PC open Desktop → IP Configuration and type its address and netmask 255.255.255.0.

  • ☐ Packet Tracer installed
  • ☐ 2960 switch and four PCs cabled to the right ports
  • ☐ Addresses set from the plan

Hint: Wait until the link lights turn green; a new switch takes a few seconds.

Question: Which cable type joins a PC to a switch? (two words, as Packet Tracer names it, without "Copper")

2. Make the VLANs

Click the switch, open the CLI tab, and type:

enable
configure terminal
vlan 10
 name STAFF
vlan 20
 name GUEST
interface range fa0/1 - 12
 switchport mode access
 switchport access vlan 10
interface range fa0/13 - 20
 switchport mode access
 switchport access vlan 20
end
show vlan brief
  • ☐ Both VLANs exist with names
  • ☐ Ports 1-12 in VLAN 10, 13-20 in VLAN 20
  • ☐ show vlan brief checked

Hint: interface range sets many ports at once. Unused ports 21-24 stay in VLAN 1: you lock those down in lab 6.

Question: Which command lists every VLAN and its ports?

3. Prove the separation

From staff-1's Command Prompt, ping staff-2 (192.168.10.12): it answers. Ping guest-1 (192.168.20.31): it fails.

Now the real test: change guest-1's address to 192.168.10.99, as if a visitor guessed the staff range. Ping staff-1 again. It still fails, because the switch never forwards VLAN 20 frames to VLAN 10 ports. Put the address back.

  • ☐ Staff to staff works
  • ☐ Staff to guest fails
  • ☐ A guest using a staff address still can't reach staff

Hint: VLANs separate at layer 2. Choosing a different IP address doesn't move you into another VLAN: the port decides.

Question: With a staff-range address on a guest port, can guest-1 reach staff-1? (yes or no) Question: What decides which VLAN a computer is in: its IP address or its switch port?

4. Where next

One switch, two separate networks. Guests still need the internet, and that needs a router: lab 5 joins the VLANs on purpose, with control.
