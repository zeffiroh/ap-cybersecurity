A role isn't just a convenience for onboarding new hires, it's a pre-committed answer to "how much damage does one phished account cause," decided in advance, before any attack happens. A tightly scoped support-agent role turns a phishing success into a contained incident; a role padded with "just in case" permissions turns the exact same phishing email into a much larger breach. Key terms to lock in: - Permission, a single narrow grant to perform one action on one resource type. - Role, a named bundle of permissions assigned to users as a unit (RBAC). - Least privilege, granting a subject only the permissions it needs, nothing more. - Privilege creep, the gradual, unnoticed accumulation of unnecessary permissions over time. - Role review / recertification, periodic re-confirmation that role assignments are still justified, used to counteract privilege creep. - Separation of duties, splitting a sensitive process across multiple roles so no single role can complete it alone.
