1. Brief

The situation. In cybersecurity, organizations must assess risk and decide how to respond to it. Risk is typically calculated by multiplying the Likelihood of an incident by its Impact: $$\text{Risk Score} = \text{Likelihood} \times \text{Impact}$$

Where both Likelihood and Impact are scored on a scale of 1 to 5.

Once the Risk Score is determined, organizations choose one of four risk treatment decisions: 1. Avoid: Eliminate the cause of the risk (e.g., shutting down a highly vulnerable non-critical service). 2. Mitigate: Apply controls to reduce the likelihood or impact of the risk (e.g., installing a firewall or implementing MFA). 3. Transfer: Shift the risk to a third party (e.g., purchasing cyber insurance or outsourcing to a secure cloud provider). 4. Accept: Decide to live with the risk (usually because the cost of fixing it is higher than the potential impact).

Your task is to write a function solve(data) that takes a list of risk scenarios and returns a dictionary mapping each scenario's name to its risk treatment decision based on the following prioritised rules:

  1. Avoid: If the risk_score is 20 or higher AND the mitigation cost is strictly greater than 60% of the asset value (cost > 0.6 * value).
  2. Mitigate (High Risk): If the risk_score is 15 or higher AND the mitigation cost is less than or equal to 30% of the asset value (cost <= 0.3 * value).
  3. Transfer: If the risk_score is 15 or higher (and does not meet the criteria for Avoid or Mitigate above).
  4. Mitigate (Medium/Low Risk): If the risk_score is 5 or higher AND the mitigation cost is less than or equal to 10% of the asset value (cost <= 0.1 * value).
  5. Accept: For all other cases.

Lesson objective. Define likelihood, impact, and asset value in a cybersecurity context, calculate a risk score, and select and justify the most appropriate risk treatment (Mitigate, Accept, Transfer, Avoid) for a given scenario.
