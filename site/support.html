Support & Sponsorship

Topic 1.1 is free to try; one payment unlocks the whole course for good. This page is for the people who want to give more.

There are two ways to help: contribute whatever the material is worth to you, or sponsor a unit and reach the students working through it.

Contribute

Twenty-four topics, a full textbook, quizbanks, labs, and the activity library take real upkeep: hosting, licensing the diagrams and photographs, and the ongoing work of checking every unit against the current framework each time it changes.

If the material earned a place in your classroom and you have a budget for materials, a contribution goes directly into that work.

You choose the amount; there is no suggested figure and no tier. A contribution is separate from unlocking the course.

Audience

The readers here are secondary-school students working through an AP Cybersecurity course and the teachers choosing their materials. It is a narrow, self-selecting group: difficult to reach through general advertising, and already committed to the subject rather than merely curious about it.

What makes it worth sponsoring is not volume but position. Someone finishing the unit on application security has just spent hours on secure coding and cryptography. The units are the targeting.

Sponsorship

Tier one, course sponsor

One organisation, credited across every unit and at the close of the textbook, sold by the semester. It is the only placement that appears inside the book itself, and it is exclusive: there is one, and while it is taken no unit is sold separately over it.

Tier two, unit sponsor

A single resource card at the foot of every topic page in one unit, in the course's own card format and permanently labelled as sponsored. Written as a genuine next step for a reader who has just finished that material, a lab platform, a certification, a summer programme, a degree. Five units, sold separately.

Terms

  • Text and a link. No images served from elsewhere, no tracking pixels, no third-party scripts, no retargeting, the audience includes minors on school networks, and anything that phones home is filtered there in any case.
  • Because nothing is loaded from an ad network, placements are not blocked, and there is no consent banner for anyone to dismiss.
  • Copy is written to be useful to a reader at that point in the course, and is agreed with you before it goes up. Anything unsuitable for a classroom is declined.
  • Sponsorship buys the placement and nothing else. It does not influence course content, the textbook, or which tools the material teaches.
  • No sponsor receives student data. Measurement is a count of unit views and holds nothing about any reader.
  • Every placement is labelled as sponsored, permanently and visibly.
