How it works. The video plays silently while you read each line in your own words. Pause whenever you need to: the video waits for you, so your voice stays in step.

A mistake? Stop, pick the chapter before it in Start from, and record again. Recording from a point replaces everything after it and keeps everything before it.

Keys: R record, Space pause or resume, S stop and keep.

When the last chapter is done, press Make the final video. The voiced video is saved next to the silent one, with the sound levelled for YouTube.
