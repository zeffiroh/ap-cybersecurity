Halcyon Aero - Information Security Policy (excerpts)

4.2  Company data may only be stored on company systems. Personal cloud storage
     (personal Google Drive, Dropbox, FileCrate and similar) is not allowed.
5.1  Removable media (USB drives) may be used only with written approval from IT.
     Files marked CONFIDENTIAL may never be copied to removable media.
6.4  Remote access through the VPN is allowed for work purposes only.
7.3  When an employee gives notice, IT reviews their access within 2 working days
     and removes anything they do not need for their remaining duties.
9.1  Suspected misuse is reported to Security, who preserve evidence and involve
     HR and Legal before any conversation with the employee.
