From: Facilities
To: All staff
Subject: Visitor policy

Reminder to all staff about the new visitor policy.
Every visitor must sign in at the front desk and wear a badge.
Anyone without a badge should be walked back to reception, politely.
Do not hold the side door open for people you do not know.
Tailgating is the easiest way into a building, so please stay alert.
Head office will run unannounced checks during the next two weeks.
Each department lead will get a short report afterwards.
Many thanks to the facilities team for fixing the lobby turnstile.
As always, report lost badges the same day so they can be disabled.
Repeated forgotten badges will need a manager's sign-off.
Guests for the Friday event are already on the list.
If in doubt, call security on extension 4040.
Nothing in this memo replaces the full policy on the intranet.

- Facilities
