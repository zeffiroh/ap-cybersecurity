Password policy proposals, IT committee, draft 3

Policy A: a passphrase of 4 words picked at random by dice from the EFF long list (7,776 words).
Policy B: exactly 8 characters, lowercase letters a-z only, chosen at random.
Policy C: exactly 10 characters, chosen at random from the 94 printable keyboard symbols.

Question from the principal: which is strongest against an offline guessing attack,
and is a 4-word passphrase really weaker than a short complex password?
