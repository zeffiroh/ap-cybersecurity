# Refund and delivery policy

**Aayat-AP-Prep** · Last updated 24 November 2026

## Delivery

Everything is digital. Nothing is shipped.

| You buy | You get it | How |
|---|---|---|
| Course, Labs, Seminars pass, a seminar | Within about 10 minutes of paying | Sign in with the email you paid with; it unlocks on its own |
| Tickets | Within about 10 minutes | Added to your ticket balance on the same email |
| Book (PDF) | Straight away | Razorpay: the PDF password is shown and emailed. Gumroad: the download link |

If it hasn't arrived within 1 hour, email aayatmahnoor@proton.me with the email you paid with and the order number.

## Refunds

| Product | Refund | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| Course | Full refund within 1 month of buying | If you have finished fewer than 3 topics' tests |
| Labs | Full refund within 1 month of buying | If you have opened fewer than 2 cases |
| Seminars yearly pass | Full refund within 1 month of buying | If you have not handed in or earned a certificate for any seminar |
| A seminar bought with tickets | Tickets back within 1 month | If you have not handed in or earned its certificate |
| Tickets (unused) | Refund of unused tickets within 1 month of buying | Pack discounts are worked out again at the single price for the ones you used |
| Book (PDF or EPUB) | No refund once the password or download has been given | Unless the file is broken and we can't fix it |

- A refund goes back the way you paid, within 5 to 7 working days of our approving it (your bank may take longer).
- When a refund is made, the access it paid for is removed.
- We don't refund when an account broke the [Terms](terms.html) (for example, shared answers or sign-in).
- Early-bird and code prices are refunded at the price you paid.

## How to ask

Email aayatmahnoor@proton.me from the address you paid with, with the order number and one line on why (it helps us improve, and it
never decides the answer if the conditions above are met).

## Cancellations

- Live classes: cancel until 2 hours before it starts and your tickets come back. If we cancel, they always come back.
- Nothing renews by itself, including the yearly Seminars pass, so there is nothing to cancel.
