\chapter*{About the Author}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{About the Author}

I studied Mathematics, and Biology from a computational perspective, and began teaching with the opportunity to impart that knowledge to children, and in turn learn about how learning works. I taught, and at the time of writing this book continue to teach, AP Biology and AP Environmental Science at Gide.AI -- courses that are collegiate level and yet aimed at high-schoolers. Through that experience, and a push to begin technical courses, I developed this book -- and I did not expect, going in, how much the mathematics and the biology would end up mattering for a book about computer security.

The past year of teaching gave me an unusual amount of exposure: from how a learning management
platform actually works underneath its interface to how the students moving through it day to
day retain and learn. Building and running Advanced Placement courses at that level -- watching a framework document turn into weeks of actual instruction -- taught me how a curriculum is structured underneath its own syllabus, and how an introductory undergraduate-level course earns that description.
This book is written for AP Cybersecurity students: a curated reading reference built along College Board's well-defined and designed curriculum, so that it can be taught in real life. AP Cybersecurity is one of College Board's AP Career Kickstart courses -- courses built to connect college-level academics with real-world professional skills and industry credentials for high-demand careers.
