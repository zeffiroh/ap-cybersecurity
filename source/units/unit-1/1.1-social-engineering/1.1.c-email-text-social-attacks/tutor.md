# 1.1.c tutor notes

Notes for the pre- and post-lecture tutor posts.

## The intuition to hold onto
A useful way to hold email, SMS, and social media together in your head: each channel is effective for a *different* reason, not the same reason wearing a different costume. Email works because it looks institutional. SMS works because it feels personal and urgent, a text landing on the phone in your pocket carries a different weight than an email in a crowded inbox. Social media works because it borrows your existing trust in a specific person or brand you already follow. An attacker who has been rebuffed on one channel doesn't give up; they just switch to a channel where your guard is calibrated differently.

## The big picture
The multi-channel "pre-warning" pattern described here, a text that says "expect an email from us" followed by the actual phishing email, is worth sitting with, because it's a small example of a much bigger idea you'll see again in this course: attackers build *social proof* across independent-seeming signals to defeat verification. The single defense that generalizes across every channel is the same one from 1.1.a: verify through a path the message itself didn't give you. Look up the number yourself. Don't use the one in the text.
