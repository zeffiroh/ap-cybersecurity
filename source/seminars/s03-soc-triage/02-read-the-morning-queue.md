## 2. Read the morning queue

It's Wednesday 11 November, the first busy week of the wedding season at Lotus Lake Hotels. Hotels are a favourite target at this time of year: bookings, advances to suppliers and guests' cards all move fast. You have ten alerts. Your job is to close what is noise, finish what is already handled, and escalate only what needs a person now.

Read the queue once end to end before you decide anything. Then hash the evidence, so you can show later that nothing changed while you worked.

- [ ] Hash every evidence file
- [ ] Read all ten alerts
- [ ] Which alerts are reported emails, and which come from machines?
- [ ] Which one has money and a deadline today?

> Hint: Look for money and a deadline. Attackers who can't get past the mail filter often go after the payment process instead.

?> How many alerts are in the Wednesday queue? :: 10

?> At what time (IST) does today's payment run close? (HH:MM) :: 14:00
