## Opening

Welcome. In India you must report certain incidents to CERT-In within six hours. This is an exercise at a made-up lender, Kaveri Microfinance. Check the current rules before real use.

## 1. The setting

Read the setting so you know the company, the date and the time zone. Then open the evidence in the case folder.

On screen: the step text and the tool.

## 2. Start the clock

Every breach clock starts when you noticed, not when the attack began and not when you finished investigating. Read the analyst's note and the README. The analyst reviewed the WAF digest at 10:20 IST. IST is UTC plus five thirty, so write the detection time in UTC and work out when the six-hour report is due.

On screen: the step text and the tool.

## 3. File with CERT-In inside six hours

Now file with what you know. Data breach fits best here: personal data was taken by someone who should not have it. Partial facts are fine; say what you do not know yet in the symptoms. The form checks your times against the evidence. After you file, advance the clock: replies and new facts arrive in the inbox. Answer them, and send an update whenever the facts change.
