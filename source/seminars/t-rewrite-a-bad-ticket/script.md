## 2. A ticket nobody can act on

Read helpdesk ticket 88213 and last night's handover. Both are the kind of notes analysts get every day, and both leave out almost everything. A useful note answers five questions: what fired, what I checked, what I found, what I do not know, and what happens next.

On screen: the step text and the tool.

## 3. Write the triage note

Now rewrite it as a triage note for alert AL-2203. The report builder checks each section as you type. It wants an alert ID, times with a time zone, the exact searches you ran, at least one gap, a disposition and an owner. Keep one time zone, write where you saw each fact, and put guesses under what I do not know yet. That section is never empty. Here: did the other two people open the invoice, and was anything copied out?
