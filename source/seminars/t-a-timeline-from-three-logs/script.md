## 2. Rebuild the night on one timeline

Every log has its own format and its own idea of time, so first agree one clock. Here everything is UTC, and India is five and a half hours ahead. The merged timeline reads all four sources together, sorted by time. Narrow it to the window the alerts point at, then star the events that tell the story: the VPN login from an odd place, the first remote logon, the process that should not have started. The gaps matter too. A quiet hour in the middle is a question to ask, not a hole to ignore.
