# Lecture script: A risk register row a board understands

Free tutorial · about 15 minutes of lab work · the video runs about 6 minutes

Read it aloud while the demo plays (add `&demo=1` to the lab link), or record your own screen with the Teaching Desk. The demo's captions are this text, word for word.

## Opening

Welcome. A risk register is only useful if a board can decide from it. In this tutorial you fix a register that has holes and add the risks the threat model found.

## 1. The setting

Read the setting so you know the company, the date and the time zone. Then open the evidence in the case folder.

On screen: the step text and the tool.

## 2. Build the risk register

The first register has no owners, no treatment, and a high risk marked accepted with nobody's name on it. Load it into the risk tool. Every row needs a likelihood, an impact, an owner, a treatment and a date. Multiply likelihood by impact: fifteen or more is high. Accepting a risk is a real choice, but it belongs to someone senior enough to pay for the consequence, so a high risk accepted without sign-off gets flagged. Fix the rows, then add the new risks. When every row passes, you have a register someone can act on.
