## 3. The attack tree: the cheapest way in

An attack tree asks one question: what's the cheapest way to reach the goal? OR means any child will do; AND means all are needed. Costs are rough effort. The tool finds the cheapest path. Then cut it, and see what becomes cheapest next.

- [ ] What's the cheapest path now, and its cost?
- [ ] Fix it (say how): what's the cheapest path after that?
- [ ] Keep going until the cheapest path costs more than the money it would get

> Hint: Signing the webhook removes the cost=1 branch entirely. Then "change the amount in the app" is cheapest, until the server looks up the amount itself.
